Arrow Reveal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Overview

Arrow Reveal is a free puzzle game for iPhone and iPad published by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, boosters, stars, achievements and settings are saved only on your device. We do not collect or receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The game is funded by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as your advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system and ad interactions. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in the Apple "Allow Tracking" prompt; otherwise you see non-personalised ads. In regions that require it, a consent form lets you choose how your data is used, and you can change your choice at any time. Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Photos

If you tap "Save as wallpaper", the game asks for permission to add an image to your Photos. It only adds the picture you chose; it never reads, uploads or deletes your photos.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the game schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, reset your advertising identifier, turn off notifications, and remove Photos access in iOS Settings.
